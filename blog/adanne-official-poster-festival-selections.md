**Debut Feature Film Adanne Unveils New Poster, Announces Selections to AAIFF Rwanda and Women of the Lens Festival London**

Melon House Production is proud to reveal the official poster for Adanne, the company's debut feature film, alongside news of two festival selections: the All African Independent Film Festival (AAIFF) in Kigali, Rwanda, and the Women of the Lens Festival in London.

![Adanne Official Poster](/images/Adanne_Official_Poster.jpg)

## About Adanne

Adanne tells the story of three generations of Igbo women navigating love, duty and family tension, centered on a young woman caught between her creative ambitions and the traditions she's expected to uphold. Performed in English and Igbo, the film is written and produced by Gold Gerry, produced by Lotanna Nwose, and directed by Orobosa Ikponmwen in her feature debut. The cast includes Mmesomachi Chilaka in the title role, alongside Onyinye Odokoro, Kelvinmary Ndukwe and Jennifer Umenwa. Shot on location in Ibadan, Nigeria.

The film examines how patterns of behaviour, belief and survival pass through generations of women, and looks closely at the quiet negotiations, sacrifices and tenderness that shape mother-daughter relationships within a shared home.

## Festival Selections

Adanne has been selected to screen at AAIFF in Kigali, Rwanda, taking place October 15 to 17, 2026. The film has also been accepted into the Women of the Lens Festival in London, screening in November 2026. Both selections mark early stops on Adanne's international festival run, with further screenings planned across the EMEA region as the film builds toward theatrical release.

## About Melon House Production

Melon House Production is an independent film and entertainment company telling culturally rooted African stories for global audiences through film, production and creative services. Adanne is its debut feature film.
